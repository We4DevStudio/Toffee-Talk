About Toffee And Talk

Toffee And Talk is more than just a café. It is a cozy space designed for conversations, connections, and comforting coffee experiences.

Located in the heart of Jayanagar, our café brings together freshly brewed coffee, indulgent desserts, and warm hospitality in an atmosphere where friends meet, ideas flow, and memories are created.

Come for the coffee. Stay for the conversations.
